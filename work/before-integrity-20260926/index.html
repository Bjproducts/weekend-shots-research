Early repeated differences

  • The supported within-player totals separate sharply by construction: saved hits average 4.00 shots; the nine same-player misses with supported counters average 0.89.

    Descriptive only. This confirms the comparison cohort but does not identify a pre-match cause.

  • Long minutes did not guarantee 2+ shots: at least five same-player misses have supported 90-minute appearances (Joao Pedro, Ante Budimir, Rafael Navarro, Guilherme Augusto and Lionel Messi).

    Hypothesis: opportunity quality, team volume and player share may matter more than minutes after a basic playing-time threshold. Hit-side context is still missing, so this is not yet tested.

  • Supported team context now covers 15 hits and 8 same-player misses. Hit teams averaged 13.2 shots and selected players averaged a 29.2% share; misses averaged 11.5 team shots and a 9.0% player share.

    Hypothesis: player share may separate outcomes more strongly than team volume alone. This is descriptive, outcome-selected evidence and is not a threshold or betting rule.

  • A goal or creative return did not ensure repeated shooting: Ante Budimir scored with his only shot; Joao Pedro had a goal and assist from one shot; Lionel Messi had two assists from one shot.

    Hypothesis: avoid using goals or general attacking involvement as a substitute for shot-volume evidence.
