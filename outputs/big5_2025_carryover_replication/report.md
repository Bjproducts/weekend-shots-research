# Big-five early-season previous-season carryover replication v1

This is a **new early-season variant**, not the original same-season-only model. It fills missing eight-match and five-start history from the immediately previous season under the frozen 180-day, same-team rules.

Coverage: 168 weekend fixtures; 1848 home-starter samples; 19 qualifying environments.

Pattern A: **6/13 (46.2%)**. Pattern B: **3/5 (60.0%)**.

## Limits

- This replication was selected after the 2026 carryover replay, so it is new data but not a prospective trial.
- FotMob provider definitions require validation and may differ from StatsBomb or SportsAPI.
- Historical market odds are not used; fixed prices are hypothetical.
- Actual historical starting lineups are treated as known at lineup time.
- Current lineups are actual historical starters, so this is a retrospective lineup-time replay.
- UTC fixture dates define the weekend buckets.
- Hit rates describe this sample and are not guaranteed future win rates.
