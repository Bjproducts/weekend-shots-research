## Limits

- This is retrospective historical validation using actual starting lineups as if known at lineup time.
- The ranking design was developed using the two later seasons, but the target 2024/25 outcomes have not been used to tune it in this project.
- Fixed odds are hypothetical and not historical market prices.
- Tier C fixtures remain research rankings only.
- Multiple players or fixtures in one weekend are correlated.
- Hit rates are descriptive historical estimates, not guaranteed probabilities.
