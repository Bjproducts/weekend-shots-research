# Big-five early-season carryover: combined assessment

## Decision

Do **not** promote the previous-season carryover variant to the live weekend formula yet. The first four-week replay looked strong, but the unchanged second-season replication did not confirm it.

| Dataset | Target fixtures | A | B |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2026/27 four-week replay | 156 | 13/16 (81.2%) | 2/2 (100.0%) |
| 2025/26 untouched replication | 168 | 6/13 (46.2%) | 3/5 (60.0%) |
| **Combined** | **324** | **19/29 (65.5%)** | **5/7 (71.4%)** |

Approximate descriptive 95% Wilson intervals are 47.3%–80.1% for A and 35.9%–91.8% for B. These intervals do not account for correlated players, fixtures or leagues.

The two seasons point in materially different directions. A moved from 81.2% to 46.2%; B has only seven combined picks. Consequently, neither the first-season percentage nor B's small-sample rate should be treated as the expected live win rate.

## What repeated

- The rule remained selective: 29 A picks from 324 target fixtures across eight weekends.
- Winning selections in both samples had higher previous-five average shots than misses, but this is descriptive and was observed after the outcomes.
- LaLiga happened to return 5/5 A hits across the two samples, but that league sample is far too small for a league-specific rule.

## What failed to repeat

- The strong overall A hit rate.
- The idea that stricter B conditions reliably solve A's misses. The replication B result was 3/5.
- Cross-league consistency: in the replication, Premier League A was 0/2 and Bundesliga A was 0/2.

## Next research action

Keep the original same-season formula and this carryover variant separate. Before changing thresholds, inspect the seven replication misses against the six hits for role/position, set-piece dependence, current-season versus carried starts, and whether the player remained a true primary shooter after the season boundary. Any proposed refinement must then be frozen and tested on a third untouched sample.

No historical market odds were used. Starting lineups are actual historical lineups treated as if known at lineup time. This is retrospective research, not a profit claim.
