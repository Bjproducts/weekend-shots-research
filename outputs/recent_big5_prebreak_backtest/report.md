## Why

- Premier League finished the window at Matchweek 5: at most four earlier results.
- La Liga finished at Matchday 7 because of a midweek round: at most six earlier results.

- Serie A and Ligue 1 were also below eight earlier results.

Therefore every fixture fails the first history gate. Looking up player outcomes cannot change eligibility.
